# Helios Bundle Draft — Rules Sheet

## What this is

A two-round draft for Yu-Gi-Oh! played from a host-curated card pool (a "cube"). You draft hidden bundles, then hidden single cards, then build a deck.

## The draft at a glance

- **Two rounds.** Round 1 drafts bundles; Round 2 drafts single cards.
- **15 picks per round, 30 picks total** — every player makes exactly 30 picks, no matter how many people are at the table.
- **Round 1 passes LEFT. Round 2 passes RIGHT.**
- **Everything is hidden.** Your picks go into a private binder. Nobody sees what you take — they can only guess from what's missing when a pack reaches them.
- **Packs are always 15 items.** Pack size never changes with player count.

## Round 1 — Bundles

- Each pack holds 15 hidden bundles.
- A bundle is a ready-made package: a core of Main Deck cards plus any Extra Deck bosses built into it.
- Bundles vary in size — from small 2-card packages to big archetype cores. **Every bundle contains at least 2 Main Deck cards.**
- When you pick a bundle, you get every card in it.

## Round 2 — Singles

- Each pack holds 15 individual cards: generic, high-utility Extra Deck monsters and universally playable Main Deck spells, traps, and tech.
- One pick = one card.

## While you draft

- **Your pool is sorted for you:** Main Deck cards and Extra Deck cards appear in separate tabs.
- **Bundle view:** you can regroup your picks into their original bundles to see which engines you've assembled. This works only for cards that came from a bundle — single cards from Round 2 are never grouped.
- **You're on a clock.** Each pick has a timer. It gets shorter as the pack empties. If time runs out, the game picks for you (it favors the highest-rarity card in the pack) — or confirms a selection you'd already made but not locked in.
- **You don't have to wait for others.** Packs come to you as soon as the player before you picks. If you're slow, packs stack up for you while faster players keep going.

## Building your deck

- **Main Deck:** at least 30 cards. No maximum.
- **Extra Deck:** at most 15 cards.
- **Everything else is your Sideboard** — no size limit.
- **No copy limit.** Run as many copies of a card as you drafted.
- **The host's pool is law.** Card legality and bans come entirely from the curated pool, not official banlists.

## Between games

- In a best-of match, you may swap cards between your deck and sideboard **card-for-card**.
- **Your Main Deck and Extra Deck sizes stay the same all match** — you can change which cards are in them, but not how many.

## Quick reference

| | Round 1 | Round 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Pack contents | 15 bundles | 15 single cards |
| Pass direction | Left | Right |
| Pick = | whole bundle | one card |
| Bundle view | yes | no |

**Every player: 15 picks + 15 picks = 30 picks.**
